# Constitution (draft) as it was before it was replaced by the SAO template on 2026-11-17

Summary: The few things that need two-thirds of a General Meeting and then SAO: ExCo and its powers, CCAs as part of the Society, accounts, removal and appeal.

**C-1.1 ExCo and its officers**
ExCo has eight elected officers with equal votes. The Constitution names the officers and what each is responsible for.

**C-1.2 Power to make Bylaws**
ExCo may make Bylaws under the Constitution. Bylaws are published within 7 days of being made, and a General Meeting can annul them.

**C-1.3 CCAs as constituent parts**
CCAs registered under the Society’s Bylaws are constituent parts of the Society and are governed by its constitution.

**C-1.4 Financial year and accounts**
The Society’s financial year, and approval of its accounts at the AGM.

**C-1.5 Removal of officers**
How ExCo officers are removed.

**C-1.6 Right of appeal**
The right of appeal against removal.
